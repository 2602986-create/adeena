Cats: Our Furry Friends

Cats are loving, playful and clever animals.
Many people keep them as pets because they are cute and calm.

Why People Love Cats

  • They are soft and cuddly
  • They keep themselves clean
  • They are fun to watch when they play

How to Take Care of a Cat

  1. Give fresh water every day
  2. Feed good cat food
  3. Clean the litter box
  4. Take it to the vet
